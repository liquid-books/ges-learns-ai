## Company Overview

**GES (Global Experience Specialists)** — leading global provider of exhibition and experiential marketing services. Founded 1939 as Manncraft in Kansas City (sign, window, small exhibit company). Rebranded GES Exposition Services 1993; shortened to GES in 2018. Serves event organizers, exhibitors, agencies, and brand marketers across US, Canada, Europe, Middle East, India.

**Key Facts:**
- **Founded:** 1939
- **HQ:** 7000 Lindell Road, Las Vegas, Nevada
- **Employees:** ~2,600–3,500+ globally
- **Revenue:** est. $535M–$879M
- **Ownership:** Private — acquired by **Truelink Capital** (LA private equity) Dec 31, 2024. Previously a segment of **Viad Corp (NYSE: VVI)** for 55 years.
- **Reach:** 75+ countries; 4,000+ live experiences/year; 150,000+ exhibitors served/year; 24 global warehouse & production facilities
- **Social:** IG @ges_global, LinkedIn gesglobal, FB GESGlobal

## Business Model & Services

Full-lifecycle event services partner serving three customer types: **organizers, exhibitors, agencies**. Core revenue as General Service Contractor (GSC) — the official on-site service provider for trade shows — plus experiential/custom exhibit design, accommodations management, and technology platforms.

### For Organizers (Show Planners / Associations)
- **Show Planning** — floor plan management, decorator services, signage, onsite production
- **Creative & Design** — environmental design, booth concepts, graphic production, wayfinding
- **Operational Excellence** — labor management, union coordination, logistics, venue operations
- **Sponsorship Consulting** — revenue generation strategies
- **Exhibitor Success** — outreach, support, satisfaction management
- **Experiential Strategy** — attendee journey design, immersive activation
- **Service Locations** — nationwide US network at every major venue

### For Exhibitors
- **Exhibitor Services** — furniture rental, carpet, flooring, signage, electrical, cleaning
- **Turnkey/Custom Exhibits** — rental booths, custom builds any size
- **Spiro Custom Exhibits** — high-end bespoke builds
- **Shipping & Logistics** — door-to-floor freight, advance warehousing, international shipping, customs
- **GES Transportation+** — round-trip domestic logistics ≤5,000 lbs (10% off material handling, no carrier wait time fees)
- **Support** — online ordering portal (ordering.ges.com), Exhibitor Resources hub

### For Agencies
- Dedicated cross-functional creative production teams
- Pre-production, budget management, collaborative planning
- Venue-specific logistics, labor forecasting and operations
- Design engineering
- On-site event and crew management
- Post-show reconciliation and labor tracking
- High-end graphics production; large-format printing; color-accurate substrates
- White-glove service model, dedicated team per project

## Key Insights for Customizing the AI Book

### Audience Profile
GES employees are **not desk-bound knowledge workers** in the traditional sense. The workforce splits into distinct personas, and the book must speak to all of them:

1. **Show/Event Operations** — floor managers, labor coordinators, venue leads. Mobile-first, deadline-driven, on-site.
2. **Creative & Design** — exhibit designers, graphic production, environmental designers. Adobe/CAD heavy, use Workfront.
3. **Sales & Account Management** — exhibition sales, organizer relationship managers, agency account leads.
4. **Logistics & Freight** — warehousing, material handling, customs, transportation coordination.
5. **Corporate Functions** — finance, HR/People & Culture, legal, marketing, IT.
6. **Tech Product Teams** — onPeak, Visit by GES product/engineering.

### Chapter Customization Guidance

- **Replace all BankUnited banking/regulatory examples** with event-industry equivalents: show floor logistics, exhibitor orders, freight manifests, labor forecasts, booth designs, RFPs, post-show reconciliation.
- **Compliance/regulatory chapter** — pivot from banking regs (FDIC, BSA/AML) to: union labor rules and jurisdiction, venue safety/OSHA, international customs and carnets, GDPR for attendee data, ESG/emissions reporting, contract compliance.
- **Excel chapters** — use event data: labor hour forecasts vs. actuals, material handling weights and drayage costs, booth inventory, show P&L, exhibitor order volumes, freight cost per show, hotel block pickup rates (onPeak).
- **Word chapters** — RFP responses to organizers, show operations manuals, exhibitor service kits, SOWs, post-show reports, safety briefings.
- **PowerPoint chapters** — organizer pitch decks, post-show recap decks, sponsorship proposals, QBRs, design concept presentations.
- **Teams chapters** — cross-timezone show teams, on-site coordination, agency/client channels, handoffs between pre-show and on-site crews.
- **SharePoint chapters** — show document libraries, venue-specific knowledge bases, design asset libraries, safety documentation.
- **OneNote chapters** — site surveys, show walkthroughs, punch lists, install/dismantle notes.
- **Change Management & Adoption chapters** — anchor to the Truelink independence moment: "first time in decades we control our own roadmap." Frame AI as part of GES's transformation. Reference the onPeak AI Smart Suite as proof GES is already doing this.
- **Mindset chapter** — lean on T.R.U.E. values. Trust (AI is a tool, verify outputs), Responsibility (own the output, AI doesn't absolve you), Understanding (people-first, AI augments not replaces), Excellence (AI raises the floor, humans raise the ceiling).
- **Tone** — warm, partnership-focused, operationally precise. Match GES's own voice: not corporate-stiff, not hype-y. Practical.
- **Use real GES vocabulary:** drayage, material handling, GSC, EAC, show floor, install & dismantle (I&D), advance warehouse, marshaling yard, booth/stand, aisle carpet, exhibitor service kit, freight targets, move-in/move-out, union jurisdiction, carnet.
- **Global framing** — 75+ countries, multi-timezone teams, multilingual needs. Copilot translation and cross-cultural collaboration is genuinely useful here.
- **Sustainability angle** — GES cares about ESG. Show how AI helps with emissions reporting, reuse tracking (Better Stands), waste reduction analysis.
